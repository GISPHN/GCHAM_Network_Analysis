about=Analyzes shortest routes on road line networks and distance or travel-time catchments from one or more origins. Saves segment-level results, origin-specific or combined reachability lines, and convex hull polygons to GeoPackage. Catchment lines use cyan tones; origin-specific lines use distinct colors based on origin names. Large combined lines are hidden by default to reduce initial rendering load. Shortest routes use magenta tones. Compatible with QGIS 3 and 4. For public health nursing teams, estimates multi-team plans to visit all destinations or maximize the number of people served and destination priority within a time limit. Patrol planning uses a prototype heuristic based on road distance and does not guarantee a globally optimal solution. When destinations remain unvisited, users can save the current plan or adjust team count, available time, and common service time within user-defined bounds. Patrol results can be exported as a multi-sheet Excel workbook.
